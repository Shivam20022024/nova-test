Scenario 1: Sign in to Masa
Open https://dev.masa.life/login.
Wait until the heading "Welcome back." is visible.
Type the test username into the field labelled "EMAIL".
Type the test password into the field labelled "PASSWORD".
If a Cloudflare box is shown under the password field, wait until it says "Success!".
Click the large "Sign in" submit button below the password field (not the "Sign in" tab at the top of the card).
Verify the page address does not contain "/login".
Verify the navigation tabs "Today", "Journey", "Care" and "More" are visible.
