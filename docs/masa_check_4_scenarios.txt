Scenario 1: Sign in to Masa
Open https://dev.masa.life/login.
Wait until the heading "Welcome back." is visible.
Type the test username into the field labelled "EMAIL".
Type the test password into the field labelled "PASSWORD".
If a Cloudflare box is shown under the password field, wait until it says "Success!".
Click the large "Sign in" submit button below the password field (not the "Sign in" tab at the top of the card).
Verify the page address does not contain "/login".
Verify the navigation tabs "Today", "Journey", "Care" and "More" are visible.

Scenario 2: Today screen content
Open https://dev.masa.life/.
Verify a heading that starts with "Good" (morning, afternoon or evening) is visible.
Verify text in the form "Day <number> · Natural cycle" is visible.
Verify the text box with the placeholder "A line for today. Private. Just for you." is visible.
Verify the "Cycle calendar" button, the "Log temperature" link and the "How you're doing" link are visible.
Do not save, dismiss or change anything.

Scenario 3: Journal Save button only enables when text is typed
Open https://dev.masa.life/.
Verify the journal "Save" button is disabled.
Type the words "draft check" into the text box with the placeholder "A line for today. Private. Just for you.". Do not press Save.
Verify the journal "Save" button is enabled.
Clear the text from that text box.
Verify the journal "Save" button is disabled again.

Scenario 4: Main navigation tabs
Open https://dev.masa.life/.
Click the "Journey" tab. Verify the page address contains "/journey" and the heading "My Journey" is visible.
Click the "Care" tab. Verify the page address contains "/care" and the heading "Care" is visible.
Click the "More" tab. Verify the page address contains "/more" and the heading "More" is visible.
Click the "Today" tab. Verify a heading that starts with "Good" is visible.
